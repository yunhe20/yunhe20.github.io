Biography

Hi, I am Yun He, a third-year CS PhD student at the University of Maryland, College Park, advised by Prof. Matthias Zwicker. I received my master's degree in computer science from Fudan University in 2023, co-advised by Prof. Yanwei Fu and Prof. Xiangyang Xue. During my master's studies, I also collaborated closely with Dr. Danhang Tang and Dr. Yinda Zhang at Google. In 2020, I obtained my bachelor's degree in computer science from Chongqing University.

My primary research interests include:

  • Video Generation and Editing, particularly for autonomous vehicles and robotics, such as LangDriveCTRL (ECCV 2026).
  • Multimodal Agents, with an emphasis on building efficient and automated agentic frameworks, ranging from a training-free framework (LangDriveCTRL, ECCV 2026) to trainable orchestration (SceneOrchestra, ECCV 2026).
  • 3D Vision, including Gaussian Splatting for object relighting (TranSplat, ICCP 2026), as well as point cloud processing for upsampling (Grad-PU, CVPR 2023) and compression (D-PCC, CVPR 2022).
